*** 329. Introduction to PyTorch 2.0 ***
**Lecture Summary: Introduction to PyTorch 2.0**

**1. Overview of PyTorch 2.0 Release**

* PyTorch 2.0 officially released, with the primary improvement focused on speed.
* The key new feature is `torch.compile`, a backward-compatible function that compiles models to optimize performance.
* After creating a model, passing it to `torch.compile` returns a compiled model that offers speed improvements during training and inference, especially on newer GPUs.

**2. Performance Gains**

* PyTorch tested `torch.compile` on 163 open-source models (from Huggingface Transformers, timm, Torchvision, and TorchBench).
* Tests used mixed precision (automatic mixed precision, Float16) and Float32 training.
* Results showed an average speed increase of 43% in training on Nvidia A100 GPUs.
* Personal benchmarks showed:

  * Larger batch and data sizes benefit more from `torch.compile`.
  * The first training epoch is slower, but subsequent epochs run faster.
  * Speed improvements observed were about 10% on local RTX 40 and A100 GPUs.
* Further exploration needed with mixed precision training, transformer architectures, and longer training runs, where bigger gains are expected.

**3. Technologies Behind `torch.compile`**

* `torch.compile` integrates several components:

  * Torch Dynamo
  * Aot Autograd
  * Torch Inductor
* Two main optimization techniques it offers:

  1. **Fusion (Operator Fusion)**
  2. **Graph Capture (Graph Tracing)**

**4. Fusion (Operator Fusion)**

* Fusion combines multiple operations into fewer operations to reduce overhead.
* Modern GPUs have very high compute power; the bottleneck is often memory bandwidth (speed of moving data between CPU and GPU).
* Fusion minimizes how often intermediate results are written to and read from memory, reducing bandwidth costs.
* Analogy:

  * Without fusion: Adding ingredients one by one and blending repeatedly.
  * With fusion: Adding all ingredients first, then blending once — more efficient.

**5. Graph Capture (Graph Tracing)**

* Graph capture records the sequence of operations in advance.
* Without it, operations are interpreted and executed one-by-one, causing overhead.
* Analogy:

  * Without graph capture: Driving in a new area, thinking about every turn as you go.
  * With graph capture: Driving in your own neighborhood, knowing the route ahead of time.
* Upfront time is spent recording the graph, but later executions are faster.

**6. Additional Resources**

* For a deeper understanding of fusion, memory bandwidth, and overhead, see "Making Deep Learning Go BR from First Principles" by Horace Hare.

**7. Other Notable PyTorch 2.0 Features**

* Faster Transformers is now stable, improving transformer-style model speed.
* Universal device setup introduced via a context manager or global setting.
* Enhanced Metal Performance Shaders improve PyTorch support on Mac.
* Domain-specific library upgrades in:

  * Torchvision
  * Torchtext
  * Torchrec
  * Torchaudio
  * And others.
